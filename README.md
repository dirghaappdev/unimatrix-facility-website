## 🌟 Visual Showcase Edition Features

1. **4-Quadrant Hero Operations Mosaic**:
   - Live visual preview of Electrical HT/LT Panel Diagnostics, Residential CHS Townships, Pump Stations, and Fire Safety Systems.
2. **On-Ground Operations Photo Gallery (आमचे प्रत्यक्ष काम)**:
   - 8 filterable photo cards with category pills (*Electrical & DG, Plumbing & Pumps, STP/WTP, Fire Safety, Lifts & Structural*).
   - High-definition photos of actual equipment (HT/LT panels, booster pumps, STP aeration bio-culture, fire hydrants, DG AMF sync, water tank high-pressure jet cleaning, elevator machine rooms, and terrace waterproofing).
3. **Interactive Inspection Lightbox**:
   - Clicking any photo opens a high-resolution inspection modal with technical checklist details and one-click quote booking.
4. **Photo Storyboard: A Day in the Life of a Facility Team (२४ तासांचे नियोजन)**:
   - Hourly breakdown from 07:00 AM morning water checks to 24/7 standby emergency teams.
5. **Photo Banners on All 6 Technical Verticals**:
   - Real-life equipment imagery integrated into Electrical, Plumbing, STP, Fire Safety, Lift AMC, and PPM service cards.
6. **Instant Dual-Language Switcher (English ⇄ मराठी)**:
   - 1-click toggle with 100% translation of every element and persistent state.
7. **Interactive Smart AMC Cost Estimator**:
   - Configurable property type, unit slider, and service checkboxes with instant WhatsApp inquiry dispatch.
